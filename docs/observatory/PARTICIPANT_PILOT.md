# Genuine participant pilot and evidence handoff

**Proposed protocol v0.1. Not approved for collection.** This small pilot checks whether genuine operators can complete the synthetic HMRC journey and whether the Observatory preserves usable evidence. It also prepares measurement of consent, handoff and support effort that the scripted [E2 comparison](../experiments/E2_HARNESS.md) did not measure. No participants have been collected under this protocol; the [500 transaction corpus #71](https://github.com/sighmoan1/govuk-delegate-starter/issues/71) remains incomplete.

Use the [research protocol](RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md), [protected ledger](GROUND_TRUTH.md) and [E2 boundaries](../VALIDATION.md) together. The pilot cannot establish population prevalence, financial savings, fraud detection or national policy. Predictions remain independent of authority, eligibility and throughput.

## Launch ownership and prerequisites

A study owner and independent evidence reviewer must be named in the restricted launch manifest before recruitment. The owner must approve participant information, consent and withdrawal handling, access and deletion arrangements, accessibility support, recruitment approach and any external vendor/model disclosures. A reviewed PR is engineering readiness only. National decisions #48–59 remain deferred; use made-up tax records and simulator identities throughout.

Freeze the deployed Git SHA, protocol digest, exact workload/arm order, seed, application and worker configuration, browser/tool/model versions, sensor configuration, feature allowlist, classifier/config digest and evidence schema before the first session. Record which sensors are disabled, unconfigured or unavailable. Optional Fingerprint requires separately approved tracking disclosure and actual vendor configuration; a missing key is a coverage gap. A public key or a returned client identifier alone is not server-verified vendor evidence.

Use an isolated database and fresh synthetic fixtures for every operator/arm. Keep evaluator and collector credentials separate, server-side and at least 32 characters. Restrict the workbench to the research network. Test unauthorized access, monitoring truth concealment, source ingestion and failure reporting before launch. Do not reuse a shared demonstration database or place credentials, participant names, raw prompts or consent documents in GitHub.

## Small pilot allocation

The proposed ceiling is **24 planned transaction intents**, deliberately a feasibility sample. Recruit four consenting operators, each completing two manual and two human-operated AI-assisted intents: 16 human-operated intents. Add four conventional scripted and four independently traced AI-operated intents. AI-browser and API routes should both be covered where tooling permits; record the realised distribution. Assistance with wording remains HUMAN_AI_ASSISTED only when the person actually operates the transaction. Record accessibility support and overrides without treating assistive technology as suspicious.

An intent is one requested service outcome. Reads, retries, polls and authority checks are linked requests, not additional transactions or participants. Record planned, attempted, completed, abandoned, unavailable, refused and unknown counts separately. Keep failures in the pilot denominator; do not replace them quietly to reach 24 successes. Scripted preparation and browser regression are engineering checks, never human evidence.

The first two human sessions are a collection feasibility checkpoint. Pause if consent/withdrawal cannot be honoured, labels leak to inference/monitoring, a correlation fails, source evidence changes, secrets/identifying content are retained unexpectedly, or an unauthorized business effect occurs. Retain the failed attempt and investigate. Restart only with a recorded fix and protocol amendment. Stop at 24 planned intents or the owner-approved session time limit, whichever occurs first; the launch manifest must set that limit. Missing AI tooling leaves a documented cohort gap.

## Tasks and genuine effort

Use the same synthetic instructions and capability scope for every comparable condition: inspect status, lodge a permitted job-expenses claim, inspect its durable receipt, inspect one deliberately excluded operation, and revoke access before a further attempt. Keep the participant's substantive choice separate from an operator's task instruction. The facilitator must never approve on the participant's behalf.

For each human task, record observed start/end and separate time spent understanding scope, consenting, moving between systems, inspecting outcome, revoking and recovering from errors. Count interactions, help requests, facilitator interventions, abandoned steps and participant takeovers. Ask the participant to explain who can act, which action is excluded and what revocation changes. Store answers as coded observations without tax content or verbatim private conversations. Unobserved timing/effort is null with a reason, never zero. Record assistance time separately from participant time.

For the incremental [#47 comparison](https://github.com/sighmoan1/govuk-delegate-starter/issues/47), B and C must use the same HMRC API, domain rules, idempotency, queue/worker budget, technical limits, tool versions and task instructions. B has independently citizen-approved service-local authority; C has Delegate authority. Counterbalance B/C and C/B order across operators with a frozen allocation. Reset only isolated fixtures between arms. A context web journey cannot substitute for B.

**Current B has fixture-driven approval/revocation and no genuine participant consent UI.** Therefore this first pilot may collect C and manual/assisted feasibility evidence, but cannot publish a causal B/C human-effort comparison. Before that comparison, implement a separately reviewed accessible B consent/revocation process with comparable scope and assurance, then freeze the amended protocol. Provider handoff is excluded from both current harness arms; measure it only after symmetric implementation. Document unavoidable topology/authentication differences. Do not count API or queue improvements as Delegate's contribution.

## Collection and independent audit

1. With `x-evaluation-key`, create a CONTROLLED run using `POST /api/evaluation/runs` with `mode`, `protocol_version`, full `source_commit`, `seed` and `study_owner` pseudonym. Keep the launch manifest and consent records in restricted evidence storage; record their hashes. Plan each intent using `POST /api/evaluation/attempts` with `run_id` and optional opaque `intent_id`.
2. For configured browser sensor collection, create a one-use enrollment via `POST /api/evaluation/enrolments` with `attempt_id`. Pass the token through the opt-in HMRC study journey; HMRC claims it server-side using its collector credential and binds its signed session. Enrollment expires after 15 minutes and the resulting session after one hour. Re-enroll explicitly after expiry; never let the browser choose a cohort. Enrollment is optional when no sensor is configured and supplies no execution truth.
3. Observe actual operation. Human provenance requires a research observer, manual-operation attestation, observer pseudonym, consent digest, timestamp and trace digest. AI execution needs an actual agent execution trace and model/tool/source version; scripted execution needs controlled-runner provenance. Use `POST /api/evaluation/attempts/{id}/verify` only after review, with the fields defined in `verifiedProvenance` in `apps/observatory/src/lib/ground-truth.ts`. A signed request, user agent or Fingerprint event cannot attest human or AI execution.
4. Independently inspect HMRC's durable claim/transaction/query state and current Delegate permissions after the task, retry and revocation. Confirm at most one committed effect, no excluded/revoked effect and the actual receipt/outcome; HTTP 200/202 or ALLOW is insufficient. Hash the minimized audit trace. Ingest the original source observations and bind exact source IDs with `POST /api/evaluation/bindings`. UNIQUE_OPERATION/REPLAY need an opaque operation ID and the verified trace digest as `effect_evidence_digest`; keep source observations and audited effects separate.
5. The reviewer audits every pilot human attestation and service effect, records disagreements and signs the evidence manifest. The current ledger supports five execution classes and cannot verify mixed/unknown execution. Retain ambiguous attempts as PLANNED with the reason in the restricted manifest; do not force a binary class. Because completion requires all planned provenance/bindings, such a run stays OPEN and is reported incomplete. Propose a separate schema change before collecting mixed-case completion if needed.

The ledger is not a participant-management or withdrawal system. Evaluator deletion removes a run's attempts/bindings/tokens/sessions but does not erase ingested feeds, external traces, consent records or vendor records. The owner must approve and test the separate deletion process for each store before launch. Adopt the research protocol's proposed maximum 30-day raw-event retention only after owner approval, set the actual deadline in the manifest and record deletion evidence. Minimize identifiers and prohibit unrelated cross-service correlation.

## Frozen evidence and reporting

Store the immutable manifest, source-event and trace digests, cohort/operator/family assignments, outcome audits, consent references, timing/help records, amendments and reviewer findings in restricted evidence storage. Keep the protected labels separate from the frozen inference allowlist: no cohort/run tags, prompts, synthetic email, outcome/authority decisions or future evidence may reach a browser-automation classifier. Monitoring shows only observed evidence and predictions; authorized research evaluation may join truth afterwards.

This tiny pilot supplies descriptive counts and collection defects, not training/calibration/holdout accuracy. Keep all attempts from an operator, intent and related scenario/agent family together. Before expansion, freeze grouped split membership and demonstrate that enough independent groups exist for training, calibration and untouched holdout; otherwise report holdout unavailable. No thresholds are tuned on pilot evaluation results and called validated. A declared-route baseline answers its declared-identity question; it does not detect unknown AI execution.

Publish a deidentified RUN record following [EVIDENCE.md](../EVIDENCE.md), with the exact deployed revision/config, planned and realised cohort counts, label and sensor coverage, errors/abstentions, incomplete runs, independent effects, effort missingness, deviations, reviewer and limitations. Report no accuracy when prediction/label coverage cannot support it, and no cost saving from timing proxies. Link the reviewed record from #71/#47 and the evidence register only after collection; leave both issues open while their acceptance gaps remain. Escalation to 500 cases requires an amended, owner-approved protocol and real independently audited participants.
